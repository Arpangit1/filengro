About Filengro

A digital agency building premium websites, smart automation tools, and growth-driven marketing solutions for Indian businesses.

Building Digital Products for India's Growing Businesses

Filengro is a digital agency that specializes in building premium websites, automation tools, and digital marketing solutions. Started by developers who understand both technology and Indian business needs, we create digital products that actually drive results.

We know that getting online can be overwhelming — choosing the right tech stack, building something that looks professional, making sure it ranks on Google, and then automating the repetitive stuff. Filengro handles all of it, so you can focus on growing your business.

Beyond technology, we also provide comprehensive business compliance services — from company registration and GST filing to PSARA licensing and ISO certification. Whether you need a stunning website or help with regulatory requirements, Filengro is your one-stop partner.

About the Founder

Filengro is a founder-led digital agency. Every website, CRM and digital tool is personally guided from the first client discussion through planning, development, delivery and ongoing support. The goal is to give Indian businesses practical, professional digital products that create real value.

Our Mission

To empower Indian startups and MSMEs with world-class digital products — from stunning websites to intelligent automation tools — at pricing that makes sense for growing businesses. We also simplify compliance so our clients can focus on what matters.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted digital agency — building an ecosystem where businesses can get everything from a professional website and automation tools to complete regulatory compliance under one roof.

Our Core Values

Code quality, design excellence, transparent communication, and on-time delivery. We believe in building long-term partnerships, not just one-off projects. Every product we ship reflects our commitment to craftsmanship.
