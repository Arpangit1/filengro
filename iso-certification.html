ISO Certification Services

Elevate your organization's reputation and meet corporate vendor pre-requisites. We assist with Quality Manual formulation, process gap analysis, internal audits, and final certification.

What is ISO Certification?

ISO (International Organization for Standardization) certifications are globally recognized marks of quality, security, environmental management, and occupational safety. For Indian businesses�especially security, housekeeping, and manpower agencies�having a valid ISO certificate is crucial to clear corporate vendor onboarding and satisfy regulatory requirements.

Popular ISO Certificates We Provide

  • ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management System - QMS): The most widely adopted standard. It ensures that your services consistently meet customer demands and regulatory benchmarks. (Highly requested for corporate contracts).
  • ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management System - EMS): Demonstrates your commitment to sustainable practices, waste minimization, and green workflows.
  • ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety - OH&S): Crucial for manpower suppliers, security contractors, and manufacturing companies. It highlights that employee workplace safety is managed systematically.
  • ISO 27001:2022 (Information Security Management System - ISMS): Essential for IT startups, SaaS providers, and consulting firms handling confidential client records.

Strategic Value for Corporate Growth

Many corporate contracts and vendor registrations state: "The vendor must possess a valid ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 certification." Lacking these certificates can lead to exclusion from vendor lists and premium contracts. Filengro ensures you get accredited from NABCB-recognized or JAS-ANZ-recognized certification bodies for full legal validity.

Documents Required for ISO Certification

Getting your organization certified requires minimal documentation on your part:

  • Certificate of Incorporation, LLP Deed, or GST Certificate
  • PAN Card of the Business Entity
  • Letterhead and Visiting Card of the Director
  • Scope of Business Activities (Brief description of services provided)
  • Invoice or Purchase Order copies as proof of service delivery
  • Organization structure and employee strength list

Our Certification Audit Procedure

We guide you step-by-step to avoid compliance risks:

  1. Gap Analysis: We inspect your existing service operations against the desired ISO standard.
  2. Documentation Draft: We prepare the required Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), Quality Manuals, policy charts, and records templates.
  3. Implementation Guidance: We train your team to adopt the newly established processes and maintain logsheets.
  4. Internal Audit: We run a trial audit to identify errors or gaps.
  5. External Registrar Audit: We arrange the audit with the accredited certification body registrar and coordinate responses to queries.
  6. Issuance: The ISO Registrar issues the certificate for a standard period of 3 years (subject to annual surveillance audits).
