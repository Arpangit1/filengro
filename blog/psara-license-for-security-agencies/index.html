PSARA License for Security Agencies: Complete Guide for 2026

24 May 2026 2 min read

 Starting a private security agency in India requires proper legal authorization under the PSARA Act.

Without a valid PSARA License, operating a security guard business can create serious legal and compliance issues.

What is PSARA?

PSARA stands for: 

Private Security Agencies Regulation Act

This law regulates private security agencies operating in India.

Every security agency providing guards, bouncers, or surveillance staff must obtain PSARA approval from the concerned state authority.

Why is PSARA Important?

A PSARA License helps:

  • Build client trust
  • Participate in government tenders
  • Operate legally
  • Avoid penalties and shutdown risks

Many corporate clients and factories only work with PSARA-compliant agencies.

Basic Requirements for PSARA License

Generally required documents include:

  • Company registration documents
  • GST registration
  • Office address proof
  • Director KYC
  • Police verification
  • Security training tie-up
  • Affidavits and declarations

Common Challenges

Many agencies struggle with:

  • Documentation errors
  • State-specific rules
  • Police verification delays
  • Training compliance

This is why professional compliance support becomes important.

PSARA and Government Tenders

Most security-related tenders require:

  • Valid PSARA License
  • EPF registration
  • ESI registration
  • Labour compliance

Without these, agencies may face rejection during technical evaluation.

Final Thoughts

PSARA compliance is the foundation of a professional security agency business in India.

Agencies that maintain proper compliance systems can secure better clients and larger contracts.
