Shop & Establishment Registration

Secure your Shop Act registration certificate from the state labor department. Mandatory for operating physical business outlets, commercial offices, or logistics warehouses.

What is Shop & Establishment Registration?

The Shop and Establishment Act is a state-specific law in India enacted to regulate the working conditions, employee wages, working hours, weekly holidays, and safety standards of commercial establishments. Every business that sets up an office, retail store, hotel, or warehouse must register under the state labor department within 30 days of starting operations.

Who Needs a Shop Act License?

This registration is mandatory for any commercial establishment employing workers or operating commercial premises, including:

  • Retail shops, outlets, and supermarkets
  • Corporate office premises and co-working cabins
  • Restaurants, cafes, food stalls, and hotels
  • Warehouses, shipping yards, and logistics centers
  • Service centers, theaters, and amusement parks

Key Benefits of Shop Registration

  • Legal Business Proof: Serves as a vital government proof of address and existence for sole proprietors.
  • Current Bank Account: Banks require the Shop Act Certificate (or Gumasta license) to open business current accounts.
  • State Scheme Benefits: Access to state subsidies, loan packages, and startup registrations.
  • Avoid Fines: Prevents administrative audits, closures, and penalties by local labor inspectors.

Documents Required for Standalone Filing

Document requirements are state-specific but generally include:

  • Aadhaar Card and PAN Card of the Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  • Passport-size photo of the owner
  • Proof of business address (Rent Agreement, Consent Letter, Utility Bills)
  • Photo of the front facade of the shop/office displaying the name banner
  • Employee details (if applicable) and wage categories
